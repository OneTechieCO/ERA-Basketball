# ERA Basketball — Reel Design

## Visual Style
- Clean sports-data presentation
- Hoopers Stats-inspired animation
- Midnight navy background
- Orange branding accents
- Cream and white text

## Shot Charts
- Yellow dots: Made shots
- Gray dots: Missed shots
- Animate dots with narration
- Use verified shot locations when available
- Label illustrative locations clearly

## Reel Sequence
1. Player and era introduction
2. Verified historical statistics
3. Hypothetical era matchup
4. Python engine simulation results
5. Animated statistics and visualizations
6. Explanation of simulated outcomes
7. ERA Basketball branded outro

## Branding
- Centered ERA Basketball wordmark
- Glowing orange court-symbol app icon
- No older basketball logo
- Closing CTA:
  Follow ERA Basketball for more sims

## Production
- Vertical 9:16 design for Reels and Shorts
- Narration synchronized with graphics
- Remotion animation and rendering
- Clear distinction between historical facts
  and hypothetical simulation results
